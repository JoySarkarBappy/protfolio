Research & projects

Work in progress

Undergraduate thesis · ongoing

Natural fiber reinforced epoxy composites

Jute and water hyacinth fibers in an epoxy matrix, with hybrid formulations optimised through response surface methodology and machine learning. The target is a lightweight, recyclable panel material for vehicle and building interiors — built from two fibers Bangladesh has in abundance, one of them a river weed nobody wants.

  • Jute
  • Water hyacinth
  • Epoxy matrix
  • RSM
  • Machine learning
  • Recyclability
Major project

PGM-free diesel exhaust aftertreatment

A four-stage aftertreatment train — oxidation catalyst, particulate filter, selective catalytic reduction and ammonia slip catalyst — designed without platinum group metals, using materials that can be sourced inside Bangladesh.

Fly ash geopolymer substrates carry a rice husk ash silica washcoat and Cu–Mn–Ce mixed oxide catalysts, with Cu-zeolite for SCR, a fibre fleece filter, and a CO₂ capture cartridge at the end of the line.

  • DOC
  • DPF
  • SCR
  • ASC
  • Fly ash geopolymer
  • Rice husk ash
  • Cu–Mn–Ce oxides
  • Cu-zeolite

Jun 2026 · Abul Khair Steel Melting Ltd.

The full EAF steelmaking route

Raw material handling, electric arc furnace, ladle refining furnace, continuous casting and the rolling mill, plus the utility systems that keep them running — water treatment, fume treatment and the air separation unit — alongside quality assurance and supply chain.

Syndicate work covered leakage prevention in EAF roof and panel water cooling, and the desulphurization mechanisms used to pull sulphur down at the ladle refining furnace.
